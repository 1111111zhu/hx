# Weekly Content Rules

Apply the rules for the stage being updated after the shared Saturday-through-Friday date filter and before ranking or layout work. Never weaken a source, metric, materiality, or exclusion gate merely to fill a template.

## Shared Rules

- Filter by the actual official publication/disclosure time. Do not substitute drafting date, implementation date, event date, attachment date, or reporting-period date.
- Treat the latest supplied case PPTX/XLSX as the only visual authority. Compare every edited page's font family, font size, weight, alignment, table geometry, row height, page number, footer/source-bar gap, and blank-row behavior.
- Preserve native editable PPT tables, source text, dates, figures, qualifiers, stock codes, direct URLs, and evidence bindings. Build displayed prose by extractive editing only; use punctuation or minimal neutral connectors only when copied fragments are not grammatical together.
- Keep an untouched canonical source field and a clause-level `source_fragment_trace` for every displayed substantive statement. Do not add summaries, evaluations, implications, causal inferences, forecasts, recommendations, or procedural speculation.
- Weekly-report targets are 2 policy items, 10 industry-news items, and 10 company announcements. Industry News is evaluated item by item: keep every verified new item, report `content_shortfall` when fewer than 10 qualify, and never leave stale old-week rows that could be mistaken for current content. Evidence completion is a separate gate and may be `evidence_pending`. Policy and Company Announcements retain their stage-specific count gates. Never fabricate, use an old-week item, backfill an immaterial item, or replace the source with a weaker substitute.

## Research Results

- Use only the approved 医药新观 RSS/feed/export package. Do not bypass anti-bot controls or scrape WeChat history directly.
- Include only `深度报告` and `点评报告`; deduplicate by URL then normalized title; sort each category by publication time descending. Copy titles and dates exactly, including the year and material qualifiers.
- Use the row capacity and category allocation of the latest user-approved deck. A 20-row density applies only when that approved deck shows it; never inherit an older working copy's smaller capacity. Compare the complete qualified pool before removing old rows and record `added`, `removed`, and any `retained_older_due_to_importance` items.
- Recreate the research grid as a native editable table, then insert a complete render of that final table into worksheet 1. If the authorized source package is unavailable, leave the section unchanged and mark `blocked_source`.

## Industry Policy and Government Announcements

- For the weekly report table, select up to two strongest formally released national items; use two only when two candidates pass every gate. If fewer than two pass after the official fallback pool is exhausted, retain the prior approved policy stage and mark it blocked; never deliver a weak or out-of-window item or `本周暂无`. For a standalone request for government announcements outside the weekly report, return all qualifying items instead of padding to two.
- Use only original central-government or national-regulator sources: `nmpa.gov.cn` and direct centers, `nhsa.gov.cn`, `gov.cn`, `nhc.gov.cn`, `ndcpa.gov.cn`, `natcm.gov.cn`, and healthcare-relevant `samr.gov.cn` pages. Show the direct official detail page or file URL. An official full-text repost or attachment is allowed only as a documented rendering fallback.
- Exclude `公开征求意见`、`征求意见稿`、意见征集、咨询稿 and consultation-only notices by default. Include a draft only when the user explicitly requests consultation drafts, and never present a consultation notice as a formally issued policy.
- Prefer national normative policy, payment, procurement, regulatory-rule, guideline, clinical-service, or industry-wide administrative documents. If fewer than two qualify, use high-impact national regulator decisions such as major innovative-drug, conditional, first-in-class, first-domestic, or material-indication approvals. Do not substitute local notices, third-party rewrites, ordinary meetings, training notices, or routine product notices when stronger official items exist.
- Use `发布日期 | 文件 | 发文机构 | 主要内容`, with dates in `YYYY.MM.DD`. Match the case's native deep-purple header, light-purple/white body fills, fonts (header 16 pt bold white; body at least 14 pt), column widths, row heights, page number, footer, and source-bar clearance. Remove unused blank rows.
- Start `主要内容` with the explicit issuing agency/regulator and the material action or outcome. Remove non-substantive openings such as `经研究`、`现就`、`现将`、`通知如下` or `公告如下` when they carry no material fact. Preserve exact official wording, figures, dates, qualifiers, and scope; do not add implications, opinions, forecasts, recommendations, or procedural speculation.
- Use this user-approved structure as a language model, not reusable source text: `国家医保局就“药品限二线使用”规则对应的部分知识点调整如下：一是增加阿布昔替尼片、昂戈瑞西单抗注射液、吡格西利片、司普奇拜单抗注射液、替尔泊肽注射液、盐酸替那帕诺片、伊努西单抗注射液、英克司兰钠注射液等8种药品。二是删除赛沃替尼片。` Trace every displayed clause to an exact official quotation, page/section, or table field, and ensure the right Excel screenshot visibly covers every used fragment.
- In Excel, show the official agency/site and the raw direct official URL above the source screenshot; visible URL text and hyperlink target must be identical and in the same cell.

## Industry News

- Use only direct, openable original articles whose visible account identity is `医药魔方Info`. Reject activities, invitations, registrations, agendas, schedules, tickets, QR/attendance notices, conferences, congresses, summits, forums, salons, seminars, exhibitions, exhibitor or sponsor recruitment, livestream previews/reservations, countdowns, training courses or camps, recruitment, advertisements, sponsorships, and equivalent promotional posts before ranking. A promotional item remains excluded even if it mentions an important medical event.
- Target the 10 strongest qualified in-window articles from the currently available verified candidates. Do not require a preassembled complete pool before beginning selection. Verify account identity, publication time, direct original URL, and topmost original paragraph item by item. Reject only the item that fails those content checks. Do not require, estimate, record, or rank by reading count, likes, or other interaction metrics. Rank by `事件级别`, `产业影响`, `信息稀缺性`, then publication time; use consistent documented judgments and never use routine or promotional items merely to force ten. Missing engagement metrics never blocks the stage.
- Content and evidence are separate states. Once an item's original paragraph is verified, missing or failed screenshot capture sets that item's evidence to `evidence_pending`; it does not remove the item, revert the whole section, or restore old-week news. A fully paired PPTX/XLSX delivery still requires all selected evidence images to pass the evidence QA rules.
- Keep an audit with capture time, evidence path, exclusion reason, and the event-importance, industry-impact, and information-scarcity judgments. If a qualifying `NextPharma全球新药数据库` banner appears, include every qualified in-window match before filling the remaining places, preserve the banner in Excel evidence, and record the override; the activity-promotion exclusion still prevails.
- Keep an untouched canonical copy of exactly the topmost original paragraph. The PPT body shows only that paragraph; preserve wording, punctuation, figures, names, dates, and qualifiers exactly. Do not rewrite, shorten, reorder, add a headline, add an opening sentence, or prepend a reporting date. A date may remain only when it is part of the original paragraph. Visual line wrapping must not alter the stored text; only non-substantive layout artifacts may be removed if explicitly logged.
- Crop the source evidence from the article top through the lower black boundary of that first paragraph, including the boundary and everything above it. Exclude all later article text, recommendation/copyright blocks, action bars, and large blank canvas.
- Display selected items by publication time descending after the influence selection. Bind each PPT row, Excel left crop, original article URL, and source screenshot to one evidence record.

## Company Announcements

- Search 巨潮资讯网 and 同花顺 in parallel for A shares, plus HKEX/issuer originals for H shares. Use the exact official detail page or attachment for final text and evidence; do not cite media rewrites.
- Select exactly 10 only when 10 qualified in-window pharmaceutical, biopharma, medical-device, CRO/CDMO, medical-service, or clearly healthcare disclosures can be verified. Rank control changes, material restructuring/M&A, financing/solvency, major litigation/default, major regulatory approval, pivotal clinical results, and material earnings events ahead of routine notices; display by disclosure time descending. Do not backfill immaterial items.
- `公告主题` must be concise and no more than six Chinese characters; four characters are acceptable but not mandatory. Never use Latin letters, digits, punctuation, a longer phrase, a full disclosure title, or `三期揭盲`.
- User-specified first-tier labels are `新药获批`、`临床进展`、`并购重组`、`专利到期`、`专利获批`、`股东增持`、`股东减持`; use `海外获批` for a material overseas regulatory approval/status. Additional four-character first-tier equivalents are `控制变更`、`许可交易`、`许可终止`、`临床失败`、`产品退市`、`安全警示`、`破产重整`、`退市风险`、`重大诉讼`、`债务违约`、`审计非标`、`重大处罚`、`产品召回`、`核心停产`、`质押风险`、`股份冻结`.
- User-specified second-tier labels are `业绩预告`、`定增获批`、`股权转让`; additional four-character equivalents are `股份回购`、`股份注销`、`重大投资`、`资产出售`、`债务重组`、`资产减值`、`商誉减值`、`股权激励`、`医保纳入`、`集采中标`、`盈利预警`、`业绩快报`、`业绩公告`.
- Classify trial unblinding, phase-data disclosure, top-line results, first-patient dosing/enrollment, milestone endpoints, and material peer-reviewed clinical results as `临床进展`; never display `三期揭盲` or another unblinding-specific label.
- Put the most important official fact, amount, result, deadline, condition, or risk first in `主要内容`. Prefer direct copy/paste from the official announcement. If fragments are not grammatical together, join them only with minimal neutral connectors or punctuation; do not add analysis or unsupported causal language. Preserve source ranges, dates, and qualifiers.
- Preserve six-digit A-share and five-digit H-share codes. For earnings disclosures, retain only reporting period, revenue and year-on-year change, and attributable-net-profit and year-on-year change unless the user requests more; never calculate or infer a midpoint, percentage, or missing field.
- If the user asks for more earnings forecasts/results, apply a soft preference for qualified `业绩预告`、`业绩快报`、`盈利预警` or `业绩公告` only after stronger first-tier events. When `公司公告以半年报为主` is explicitly requested, include at least six qualified in-window half-year reports/results when available and fill the rest with the strongest other events; if fewer than six qualify, include all and report the shortfall.
- In Excel, use the exact completed native PPT row as the left screenshot, the original official-file screenshot on the right, and the direct 巨潮 URL as visible text plus hyperlink in the same cell.
