# Weekly Content Rules

Apply the rules for the stage being updated after the shared Saturday-through-Friday date filter and before ranking or layout work. Never weaken a source, metric, materiality, or exclusion gate merely to fill a template.

## Shared Rules

- Filter by the actual official publication/disclosure time. Do not substitute drafting date, implementation date, event date, attachment date, or reporting-period date.
- Treat the latest supplied case PPTX/XLSX as the only visual authority. Compare every edited page's font family, font size, weight, alignment, table geometry, row height, page number, footer/source-bar gap, and blank-row behavior.
- Preserve native editable PPT tables, source text, dates, figures, qualifiers, stock codes, direct URLs, and evidence bindings. Industry News retains its stage-specific extraction rule. For Industry Policy and Company Announcements, build displayed prose by deleting from exact official source text. Minimal neutral connectors may join fragments, but record them separately and never use them to add a fact, interpretation, causal claim, or emphasis. Do not substitute synonyms, paraphrase, or infer.
- Keep an untouched canonical source field and a clause-level `source_fragment_trace` for every displayed substantive statement. Do not add summaries, evaluations, implications, causal inferences, forecasts, recommendations, or procedural speculation.
- Weekly-report targets are 2 policy items, 10 industry-news items, and 10 company announcements. Industry News is evaluated item by item: keep every verified new item, report `content_shortfall` when fewer than 10 qualify, and never leave stale old-week rows that could be mistaken for current content. Evidence completion is a separate gate and may be `evidence_pending`. Policy and Company Announcements retain their stage-specific count gates. Never fabricate, use an old-week item, backfill an immaterial item, or replace the source with a weaker substitute.

## Research Results

- **Mandatory stage on every `all stages` refresh.** Worksheet 1 (`华鑫医药团队近期研究成果` / `Research Results`) must be rebuilt from the current 医药新观 export in the same run. Inheriting the bundled template's historical rows, copying the previous week's research section, or skipping the stage is a missed update: mark it `failed_validation`, not `completed`. Full procedure: [研究成果规则.md](../研究成果/研究成果规则.md).
- Use only the approved 医药新观 RSS/feed/export package. Do not bypass anti-bot controls or scrape WeChat history directly.
- Include only `深度报告` and `点评报告`; deduplicate by URL then normalized title; sort each category by publication time descending. Copy titles and dates exactly, including the year and material qualifiers.
- Every displayed research row must carry a publication date that falls inside the current anchor window, or be explicitly recorded as `retained_older_due_to_importance` with the reason. A row whose date cannot be traced to the current export is stale and must be removed.
- Use the row capacity and category allocation of the latest user-approved deck. A 20-row density applies only when that approved deck shows it; never inherit an older working copy's smaller capacity. Compare the complete qualified pool before removing old rows and record `added`, `removed`, and any `retained_older_due_to_importance` items.
- Recreate the research grid as a native editable table, then insert a complete render of that final table into worksheet 1. If the authorized source package is unavailable, leave the section unchanged and mark `blocked_source`; report the blocked stage explicitly instead of delivering the template rows as if refreshed.

## Industry Policy and Government Announcements

- For the weekly report table, select up to two strongest formally released national items; use two only when two candidates pass every gate. If fewer than two pass after the official fallback pool is exhausted, retain the prior approved policy stage and mark it blocked; never deliver a weak or out-of-window item or `本周暂无`. For a standalone request for government announcements outside the weekly report, return all qualifying items instead of padding to two.
- Rank qualified candidates by materiality before publication time. Use the following ordered definitions, recording the matched tier and reasons instead of relying on an unexplained score:
  1. `P1 全国制度改变`: a formally issued national rule or action that changes payment, procurement, market access, price formation, regulatory requirements, clinical-service rules, or sector-wide administration, especially when it is binding or has an explicit implementation date.
  2. `P2 全国重要规范`: a national guideline, technical standard, implementation plan, or regulator decision with broad effects on institutions, companies, product classes, or a large patient population.
  3. `P3 重大单项决定`: an exceptional innovative-drug, first-in-class, first-domestic, conditional, or material-indication approval used only after stronger system-wide candidates.
  Break ties by, in order: affected population/industry breadth, practical magnitude, binding or implementation force, information completeness, then publication time. Useful decision terms include `全国`、`施行`、`实施`、`应当`、`不得`、`医保目录`、`支付`、`集采`、`采购`、`价格`、`市场准入`、`审评审批`、`监管`、`临床服务`; keyword presence is evidence for review, never a substitute for reading the document.
- Use only original central-government or national-regulator sources: `nmpa.gov.cn` and direct centers, `nhsa.gov.cn`, `gov.cn`, `nhc.gov.cn`, `ndcpa.gov.cn`, `natcm.gov.cn`, and healthcare-relevant `samr.gov.cn` pages. Show the direct official detail page or file URL. An official full-text repost or attachment is allowed only as a documented rendering fallback.
- Exclude `公开征求意见`、`征求意见稿`、意见征集、咨询稿 and consultation-only notices by default. Include a draft only when the user explicitly requests consultation drafts, and never present a consultation notice as a formally issued policy.
- Prefer national normative policy, payment, procurement, regulatory-rule, guideline, clinical-service, or industry-wide administrative documents. If fewer than two qualify, use high-impact national regulator decisions such as major innovative-drug, conditional, first-in-class, first-domestic, or material-indication approvals. Do not substitute local notices, third-party rewrites, ordinary meetings, training notices, or routine product notices when stronger official items exist.
- Use `发布日期 | 文件 | 发文机构 | 主要内容`, with dates in `MM.DD` and no year in the table. The `发文机构` column writes one agency only; for joint notices, use the first/lead issuing agency and record the full issuer list in the audit if needed. Match the case's native deep-purple header, light-purple/white body fills, fonts (header 16 pt bold white; body at least 14 pt), column widths, row heights, page number, footer, and source-bar clearance. Remove unused blank rows.
- Choose the smallest set of complete original sentences or clauses that states what changed or was decided, whom or what it covers, and the decisive dates/numbers/conditions. Keep `主要内容` as concise as possible while remaining source-exact and complete; remove non-substantive openings such as `经研究`、`现就`、`现将`、`通知如下` or `公告如下` when they carry no material fact. Preserve exact official wording, figures, dates, qualifiers, and punctuation. Minimal neutral connectors are allowed between fragments when needed for grammar, but log them separately; do not add headings, implications, opinions, forecasts, recommendations, or procedural speculation.
- Use this user-approved structure as a language model, not reusable source text: `国家医保局就“药品限二线使用”规则对应的部分知识点调整如下：一是增加阿布昔替尼片、昂戈瑞西单抗注射液、吡格西利片、司普奇拜单抗注射液、替尔泊肽注射液、盐酸替那帕诺片、伊努西单抗注射液、英克司兰钠注射液等8种药品。二是删除赛沃替尼片。` Trace every displayed clause to an exact official quotation, page/section, or table field, and ensure the right Excel screenshot visibly covers every used fragment.
- In Excel, show the official agency/site and the raw direct official URL above the source screenshot; visible URL text and hyperlink target must be identical and in the same cell.
- The right Excel evidence screenshot or screenshots must visibly and legibly contain every character from the original source fragments used in `主要内容`; separately logged neutral connectors are exempt. Maintain a clause-level mapping from displayed text to page/section and image object plus a connector trace. If the selected text spans pages or non-contiguous regions, insert each region as a separate picture object, stacked strictly top-to-bottom with at least 12 px between adjacent images; never stitch and never place them side by side. Extend the block and shift later items down when space is short. Reopen the workbook and run `scripts/通用/证据图片版式核验.py`; overlapping rectangles, a gap under 12 px, or reversed page order fails. A body with any unsupported source word or clipped clause fails evidence validation.

## Industry News

- Use only direct, openable original articles whose visible account identity is `医药魔方Info`. Reject activities, invitations, registrations, agendas, schedules, tickets, QR/attendance notices, conferences, congresses, summits, forums, salons, seminars, exhibitions, exhibitor or sponsor recruitment, livestream previews/reservations, countdowns, training courses or camps, recruitment, advertisements, sponsorships, and equivalent promotional posts before ranking. A promotional item remains excluded even if it mentions an important medical event.
- Target the 10 strongest qualified in-window articles from the currently available verified candidates. Do not require a preassembled complete pool before beginning selection. Verify account identity, publication time, direct original URL, and topmost original paragraph item by item. Reject only the item that fails those content checks. Do not require, estimate, record, or rank by reading count, likes, or other interaction metrics. Rank by `事件级别`, `产业影响`, `信息稀缺性`, then publication time; use consistent documented judgments and never use routine or promotional items merely to force ten. Missing engagement metrics never blocks the stage.
- Content and evidence are separate states. Once an item's original paragraph is verified, missing or failed screenshot capture sets that item's evidence to `evidence_pending`; it does not remove the item, revert the whole section, or restore old-week news. A fully paired PPTX/XLSX delivery still requires all selected evidence images to pass the evidence QA rules.
- Keep an audit with capture time, evidence path, exclusion reason, and the event-importance, industry-impact, and information-scarcity judgments. If a qualifying `NextPharma全球新药数据库` banner appears, include every qualified in-window match before filling the remaining places, preserve the banner in Excel evidence, and record the override; the activity-promotion exclusion still prevails.
- Keep an untouched canonical copy of exactly the topmost original paragraph. For PPT display, the date column uses the date stated in that first paragraph as month-day only, not the WeChat/article publication date; never add a year to the displayed event date. The body column must not add a date prefix. If the paragraph begins with that date marker, remove only that leading marker from the PPT body and keep the remaining wording, punctuation, figures, names, dates, and qualifiers unchanged, except that a parenthetical segment is deleted only when that segment contains `以下简称`; keep all other parenthetical content. Other substantive dates inside the paragraph remain untouched. If the first paragraph has no explicit date, stop and ask rather than substituting the article publication date. Do not rewrite, shorten, reorder, add a headline, add an opening sentence, or prepend a reporting date. Visual line wrapping must not alter the stored text; only non-substantive layout artifacts may be removed if explicitly logged.
- Crop the source evidence from the article top through the lower black boundary of that first paragraph, including the boundary and everything above it. Exclude all later article text, recommendation/copyright blocks, action bars, and large blank canvas.
- Display selected items by publication time descending after the influence selection. Bind each PPT row, Excel left crop, original article URL, and source screenshot to one evidence record.

## Company Announcements

- Search 巨潮资讯网 and 同花顺 in parallel for A shares, plus HKEX/issuer originals for H shares. Use the exact official detail page or attachment for final text and evidence; do not cite media rewrites.
- Select exactly 10 only when 10 qualified in-window pharmaceutical, biopharma, medical-device, CRO/CDMO, medical-service, or clearly healthcare disclosures can be verified. Rank by materiality first and display the selected set by disclosure time descending. Do not backfill immaterial items.
- Use these ordered materiality tiers and record the matched tier plus the decisive fact:
  1. `A 业绩/临床/股东大额增减持`: material earnings forecasts, earnings alerts, results announcements or reports containing revenue/net-profit facts; material clinical progress such as pivotal/phase III results, primary-endpoint outcomes, top-line data, material safety results, enrollment completion, first-patient dosing for a core asset, or a material peer-reviewed result; and large shareholder increase/decrease disclosures such as 5%+ holders, explicitly large plans, or large disclosed amounts/proportions. These categories have equal top priority; within the tier prefer core-business/core-pipeline relevance, decisive quantitative results, clinical phase, shareholder scale, and magnitude of the change.
  2. `B 获批/交易/控制`: major domestic or overseas regulatory approval, material licensing, M&A/restructuring, or control change.
  3. `C 风险/资本`: financing or solvency events, default, material litigation, major punishment, recall, production halt, delisting risk, or other events with a material financial/operating effect.
  4. `D 其他重要事项`: other non-routine disclosures with a concrete, material effect.
  Routine board/shareholder procedures, personnel changes without control impact, generic progress reminders, pledge releases without risk, and boilerplate notices must not displace any higher-tier qualified item. If the source filing says the matter does not, will not, or is not expected to have a material impact on the company, exclude it regardless of topic, title, tier, or scarcity. Break ties by quantitative decisiveness, effect on the core business/pipeline, affected value or population, information completeness, then disclosure time. Useful review terms include `营业收入`、`归属于上市公司股东的净利润`、`同比`、`扭亏`、`预亏`、`关键性`、`III期`、`主要终点`、`顶线数据`、`揭盲`、`入组完成`、`首例患者`、`安全性`、`大额增持`、`大额减持`、`增持计划`、`减持计划`、`权益变动`、`持股5%以上`; keywords guide review but never replace reading the filing.
- The PPT `公告主题` must be exactly four Chinese characters. Never use Latin letters, digits, punctuation, a shorter/longer phrase, a full disclosure title, `三期揭盲`, or `业绩快报`.
- Allowed concise subject labels include `业绩预告`、`业绩公告`、`盈利预警`、`临床进展`、`临床失败`、`新药获批`、`海外获批`、`并购重组`、`控制变更`、`许可交易`、`许可终止`、`专利到期`、`专利获批`、`股东增持`、`股东减持`、`产品退市`、`安全警示`、`破产重整`、`退市风险`、`重大诉讼`、`债务违约`、`审计非标`、`重大处罚`、`产品召回`、`核心停产`、`质押风险`、`股份冻结`、`定增获批`、`股权转让`、`股份回购`、`股份注销`、`重大投资`、`资产出售`、`债务重组`、`资产减值`、`商誉减值`、`股权激励`、`医保纳入`、`集采中标`. The label names classify the event; they do not override the A-D materiality order above.
- Use `业绩预告` only for forecast/alert filings before actual results are disclosed, such as expected profit/loss, expected revenue or net-profit range, or profit-warning content. Use `业绩公告` for disclosed actual results in periodic reports, results announcements, or material earnings filings with actual revenue/net-profit figures. If the original filing title says `业绩快报`, treat that phrase only as a source keyword; when it is material enough to select, display the PPT subject as `业绩公告`, not a separate `业绩快报` label.
- Classify trial unblinding, phase-data disclosure, top-line results, first-patient dosing/enrollment, milestone endpoints, and material peer-reviewed clinical results as `临床进展`; never display `三期揭盲` or another unblinding-specific label.
- Choose the smallest set of complete original sentences or clauses that explains the event: the company/action, asset or reporting period, decisive result or amount, comparison/base, and material condition or risk. Keep `主要内容` shorter rather than fuller once those facts are covered. The first sentence must have an explicit subject; prefer the original filing's subject, and if the selected source fragment omits it, add the verified company short name as the minimal subject and log that addition separately. Use exact copy/paste from the official announcement and preserve wording, figures, dates, qualifiers, and punctuation. Minimal neutral connectors may join source fragments when needed for grammar, but record them separately; do not add a synonym, analysis, causal language, or new fact.
- Preserve six-digit A-share and five-digit H-share codes. For earnings disclosures, retain only reporting period, revenue and year-on-year change, and attributable-net-profit and year-on-year change unless the user requests more; never calculate or infer a midpoint, percentage, or missing field.
- Earnings and clinical-progress items always receive the A-tier preference above. When `公司公告以半年报为主` is explicitly requested, include at least six qualified in-window half-year reports/results when available and fill the rest with the strongest other events; if fewer than six qualify, include all and report the shortfall.
- In Excel, use the exact completed native PPT row as the left screenshot, the original official-file screenshot on the right, and the direct 巨潮 URL as visible text plus hyperlink in the same cell.
- The right Excel evidence screenshot or screenshots must visibly and legibly contain every character from the original source fragments used in `主要内容`; separately logged neutral connectors are exempt. Maintain a clause-level mapping from displayed text to page/section and image object plus a connector trace. If the selected text spans pages or non-contiguous regions, add each region as a separate picture object, stacked strictly top-to-bottom with a visible gap of at least 12 px between adjacent images; never stitch them and never place them side by side. Extend the block and shift later items down when space is short. Reopen the workbook and confirm pairwise that no image rectangles overlap; any unsupported source word, illegible figure, or clipped qualifier fails evidence validation.
